INCIDENT DETAILS
KNOW WHAT YOU’RE SEEING

Public data. Clear context.

CrimeWatch independently collects and organizes public incident records. We are not a government agency and do not originate police reports.

Coverage & timing

Publication delays vary by agency. Polk County reports may take up to 72 hours to appear. The source may omit, correct or remove records. Coverage does not include every agency or report. An incident report is not a finding of guilt, and some entries describe non-criminal activity.

Locations & categories

Map coordinates are approximate. Reports without verified coordinates remain available in the list. Locations are not verified property boundaries. Our broad categories organize reports; they do not measure neighborhood safety or predict crime.

Saved history

Collected reports are retained indefinitely. Source corrections update the displayed record; earlier collected versions remain in our private archive. Polk County reports that disappear within a checked source window are marked as no longer listed. Searches with a date range can retrieve additional records that the public source still provides. This is not a complete history of every area.

Collection schedule

Collection is scheduled for midnight, 5 a.m., and noon in America/Chicago time. Coverage varies by area: Houston provides recent mapped reports and historical files; university logs cover campus-related activity. Collection time is not publication time. Searches can also collect historical date ranges. New records appear only after the publishing agency makes them available.

Privacy

No account is needed. Search terms are sent to our server to search the saved archive. Historical date ranges may be sent to the public incident source; free-text search terms are not sent to that source. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers. Fonts load from Google Fonts, map tiles from OpenStreetMap, and map software from unpkg; those services receive normal browser connection information. The web host may retain standard access logs.

For emergencies, call 911.
