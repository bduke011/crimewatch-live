A booking is not a conviction. Everyone listed is presumed innocent. Charges are as recorded at booking and may be reduced, dismissed or never filed. The sheriff's report for a given day is usually published the following morning.
About this report

Each day's list comes from the Polk County Sheriff's Office booking report published through Polk County Today, matched to the sheriff's online inmate roster for booking photos. People who bond out before the roster is read may appear without a photo.

Names, ages, charges and agencies are transcribed from the published report and may contain errors. If you were the subject of a record and your charges were dismissed, you were acquitted, or the record was expunged or resulted in deferred adjudication, email editor@crimewatch.live with the name and booking date and the record and photo will be removed at no charge.
