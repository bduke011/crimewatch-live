About these records

This archive transcribes published Polk County Jail booking media reports. Bookings can involve multiple arresting agencies, charges or warrants. The displayed report date identifies the snapshot used; later releases or case outcomes may not be included.

Names and charges reflect the published reports and may contain errors. We do not infer current custody or conviction. Bond amounts and full police narratives are not included in this collection. Photos are not included in this initial text archive.

Available reports are checked at midnight, 5 a.m. and noon Central time. Publication can lag, and historical gaps remain. Saved entries have no scheduled expiration.
