About this roster

This page reflects the Polk County Sheriff's Office public inmate roster at the time shown. It lists people currently held at the Polk County Jail, not everyone arrested in the county; some arrestees are released before appearing, and some are held elsewhere.

Names, ages, charges, bond amounts and photos come from the sheriff's roster and may contain errors. A charge listed here is an accusation. Case outcomes are not tracked on this page.

If you were the subject of a record on this site and your charges were dismissed, you were acquitted, or the record was expunged or resulted in deferred adjudication, email editor@crimewatch.live with the person's name and booking date and we will remove the record and photo, at no charge, as required by Texas Business & Commerce Code Chapter 109.
