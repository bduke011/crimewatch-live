YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy and audience measurement

Updated September 26, 2026

Reader accounts

We store your email, optional display name, preferred community, account creation and verification dates, and most recent sign-in time. Your email verifies your account through a one-time sign-in link. A verified account is required to view reports. Signing in does not subscribe you to marketing messages. You can delete your reader account from the account page. Unverified account requests are removed after seven days during routine account requests.

App notifications

App notifications are optional and require your permission. If you enable them, we store your device notification token with your account and use your preferred community to select relevant updates. You can turn notifications off in your account or iPhone settings. Deleting your account removes its notification registrations. Native sign-in sessions expire after 30 days and are stored securely on the device.

Audience and advertising totals

We measure page views, map and Daily Arrests views, selected communities, visible ads, and clicks that open an on-site business profile. We do not send reader names or emails to advertisers. Only the site owner can access the analytics dashboard and choose to share aggregate sales reports.

Approximate daily visitor counts use a daily-changing protected hash of the connection address and browser information. We do not store the original connection address in the analytics database, use third-party advertising trackers, or associate analytics events with reader accounts. Shared networks and changing connections can cause these counts to differ from the number of people. Visitor identifiers and measured totals are retained for up to 90 days, with older entries removed as new measurements arrive.

We do not record searches, specific arrest records viewed, or precise reader locations in these analytics. Your chosen community is the only location category included in measurement. Standard hosting security logs may separately include connection addresses and requested URLs.

Use my location

When you tap “Use my location” and allow access, your device supplies a location to center the map. The map rounds it to an approximate position and shows an accuracy circle. It is not saved to your reader profile or included in our analytics or crime-report requests. You can clear it at any time. The map provider receives requests for the map tiles displayed in your viewport.

Your choice

Audience measurement uses no analytics cookies. We honor browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. You can also turn off measurement for this browser:

Essential session cookies keep accounts and admin sign-ins secure and protect forms. Your preferred community and your measurement choice may be saved in this browser.

Advertising inquiries

If you submit an advertising inquiry, your business name, contact details, and message are stored privately for the site owner to respond. They are not included in audience reports. Account sign-in emails are transactional; advertising inquiries are not automatically emailed.

Contact

For privacy or inquiry-removal requests, contact info@crimewatch.live.
