PRIVACY & DATA

Clear about your data.

No account is required. CrimeWatch does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

Searches and connections

Your searches and filters are sent over HTTPS to crimewatch.live to retrieve public records. The hosting provider may keep normal access logs, including IP addresses. Searches can request collection of available historical records. Publication delays and missing records vary by agency.

Saved on your iPhone

Saved incident copies and your preferred area stay in the app’s device storage. When alerts are off, these preferences stay on your device. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Remove reports on the Saved tab or delete the app to clear its local data.

Optional notifications

Alerts start off. Enabling them sends an Apple notification token, your chosen map center or latest device location (rounded to roughly 100 meters), area, radius, categories, summary time and time zone, and IDs of saved reports you choose to watch to CrimeWatch’s server. No name or account is required. Device location is requested only when you choose a location feature. Apple receives the token and notification text to deliver alerts. Lock-screen messages contain counts, not names or addresses.

The Settings tab has a master off switch and separate controls for each alert type. Turning alerts off and saving stops new sends after the server confirms the change, clears your stored alert preferences and watch list, and removes delivered notifications from this device. If offline, also turn notifications off in iPhone Settings until the change syncs. Already delivered messages cannot be recalled from other notification surfaces. Disabled device credentials are removed after 30 days; subscriptions inactive for 180 days are removed. When saved-report alerts are enabled, opening Saved refreshes those copies from the archive. Removing a saved report stops watching it after syncing. Alert delivery depends on source publication, network connectivity, and Apple; it is not an emergency service.

Maps and external links

Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap, which receives normal connection information and the requested map area. The My location button requests permission to center the map. That action alone does not send your location to CrimeWatch’s server; map tile requests reveal the displayed area to OpenStreetMap. If you enable current-location alerts, CrimeWatch stores your latest approximate location and its update time, replacing the previous location rather than keeping a travel history. With optional background location enabled and Always permission, iOS can update your alert area after significant movement while the app is closed. Updates may be delayed or stop after force-quitting. Nearby and daily alerts pause when location is over six hours old; saved-report updates do not depend on location. Turning off background location stops background monitoring; choosing a fixed place stops current-location updates. Turning off all alerts stops both location monitoring for alerts and server delivery once synced. You can revoke location access in iPhone Settings at any time. External source links open in a separate browser and follow the destination’s privacy practices.

Sharing

Sharing a report opens the iOS share sheet. You choose whether and where to send it.

Public records

CrimeWatch is an independent archive, not a government agency or emergency service. Reports and bookings do not establish guilt. Locations are approximate. Saved copies can become outdated; verify corrections, custody, and case outcomes with the publishing agency. For emergencies, call 911.
